# Nimbus Platform — Pricing

Nimbus has three pricing tiers: Free, Team, and Enterprise.

The Free tier includes 1 environment, 5,000 requests per month, and 7 days of log retention. It does not include Self-Hosted or Hybrid deployment modes.

The Team tier costs $49 per month per environment. It includes unlimited requests, 30 days of log retention, and access to all three deployment modes. Team tier also includes email support with a 24-hour response time.

The Enterprise tier is custom-priced and includes a dedicated support engineer, a 99.95% uptime SLA, single sign-on (SSO), and unlimited log retention. Enterprise contracts are billed annually.

Overage on the Free tier is not billed automatically — requests beyond the 5,000/month limit are rate-limited (HTTP 429) until the next billing cycle.
